ICStudio PDK metadata format, version 1

Scope: technology identity, database units, layer-purpose to GDS mapping, and relative model references.
Non-scope: executable PCells, rule decks, compact-model evaluation, foundry qualification, encrypted data, and proprietary PDK compatibility.
Encoding: UTF-8 text with LF line endings and tab-separated escaped fields.
Ordering: technology record, layer-purpose records ordered by canonical key, model records ordered by name.

Grammar:
ICSTUDIO_PDK<TAB>1
technology<TAB>NAME<TAB>DBU_PER_MICRON
layer<TAB>NAME<TAB>PURPOSE<TAB>GDS_LAYER<TAB>GDS_DATATYPE
model<TAB>NAME<TAB>KIND<TAB>SAFE_RELATIVE_PATH

Invariants:
- DBU_PER_MICRON is non-zero.
- layer-purpose pairs are unique.
- GDS layer/datatype pairs are unique.
- model names are unique.
- model paths are relative and contain no parent traversal.
